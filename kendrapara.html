Our Story

A Land of Timeless Beauty

Nestled between the Bramhani, Baitarani, and Mahanadi rivers in coastal Odisha, Kendrapara is a district of extraordinary natural and cultural richness — a secret that the world is only beginning to discover.

Home to one of India's most spectacular national parks, the world's largest nesting ground of Olive Ridley sea turtles, ancient Vaishnavite temples, vibrant festivals, and a cuisine shaped by the sea — Kendrapara is not just a destination; it is an experience that stays with you forever.

Coastal Cuisine

Taste the Ocean

Kendrapara's cuisine is a love letter to the sea. Fresh-caught prawns, crabs, and fish prepared with mustard, turmeric, and coconut in recipes passed through generations.

From the smoky chai stalls along the river ghats to the simple perfection of machha jhola over steamed rice, every meal here carries the memory of the water it came from. This is food with a soul.

Visitor Guide

Plan Your Journey

Everything you need to know before you arrive in this extraordinary corner of Odisha.

01

Best Time to Visit

October to March for wildlife and weather. February for the Olive Ridley turtle nesting season at Gahirmatha. Monsoon (June–Sept) transforms the mangroves but limits boat access.

02

Getting There

Nearest airport: Bhubaneswar (150 km). Regular buses and trains connect Kendrapara to Cuttack and Bhubaneswar. State highways are well-maintained.

03

Bhitarkanika Entry

Entry permits required. Boat tours depart from Dangamal and Khola jetties. Early morning departures (6 AM) offer the best wildlife sightings.

04

Where to Stay

Eco-resorts near Dangamal, guesthouses in Kendrapara town, and forest rest houses inside the sanctuary for an immersive overnight experience.

05

Photography Tips

Golden hour at river mouths is magical. Carry a 400mm lens for wildlife. Boat tours at dawn in still water create mirror-like reflections of mangroves.

How to Reach Kendrapara

Multiple comfortable routes connect Kendrapara to major cities in Odisha and beyond

✈️
By Air — Bhubaneswar Airport (BBI), then road via NH-53, ~2.5 hours
🚂
By Train — Kendrapara Road Station on the Cuttack–Paradip line; well connected to all major cities
🚌
By Bus — OSRTC buses from Bhubaneswar (3 hrs) and Cuttack (1.5 hrs) to Kendrapara town
🚗
By Car — NH-53 from Bhubaneswar via Cuttack. Scenic drives along the Mahanadi Delta
🛶
By Boat — Scenic river routes to Bhitarkanika from Dangamal (18 km from Rajnagar)
